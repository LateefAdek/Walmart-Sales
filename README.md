# Walmart-Sales
# Project Title: Walmart Sales
# Project Overview
This project shows a comprehensive analysis of a dataset provided from walmart, the primary objective of the analysis is to show underlying drivers of weekly,monthly and 5 years worth sales across various departments, locations and employees, the effect of holidays on sales and also trends of the course of 5 years.
# Tech Stacks
These are the following technologies and techniques used when building this dashboard:
1. Tools: PowerBI, PowerQuery, DAX.
2. Visualisation: Interactive slicers, Star Schema Model, Bar,Column and Line charts, Regional Maps and Tables.
3. Data Cleaning: ETL Processes, Data Importing using CSV file.
# Key Insights
1. They have a total of $15.75M worth of revenue over the course of 5 years (2020-2025) with a total quantity of 60,000 products with sport and outdoor equipments generating most revenue and Wincosin generating the highest revnue by states.
2. It's was noticable that most customers prefer the cash method of payment.
3. Alicia Johnson is said to be the top employee selling a total of 4077 products and revenue of over $1M.
